Ball Launch — Account Deletion

App: Ball Launch. Developer: Barsuk Studio. Last updated: July 25, 2026.

How to Delete Your Account

Ball Launch accounts are created by signing in with Google or Apple. You can delete your account and its data from inside the app:

  1. Open Ball Launch on your device.
  2. Open the Options screen (the gear button on the main screen).
  3. Make sure you are signed in — your account name is shown in the account card at the top left.
  4. Tap Delete account in that account card.
  5. Read the confirmation dialog and tap Delete account again to confirm.

On iPhone and iPad you are asked to confirm once more with Sign in with Apple. That step is what disconnects Ball Launch from your Apple ID; if you cancel it, nothing is deleted.

The deletion runs immediately and cannot be undone. When it finishes, the app signs you out and you are back to playing as a guest.

Request Deletion by Email

If you removed Ball Launch or cannot sign in anymore, send a deletion request to barsukstudio.games@gmail.com. Use the subject line "Ball Launch account deletion request" and include the email address of the Google or Apple account you signed in with, so the account can be identified. Requests are completed within 30 days.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your Ball Launch account record: the sign-in link to your Google or Apple account, plus the email address, display name, and avatar URL Ball Launch received from it. Your Google or Apple account itself is not affected — only its link to Ball Launch is removed.
  • Your player profile, including your public nickname and its reservation.
  • Every level you published to the community, together with its like counters. Published levels disappear for other players as well.
  • Every like you gave to community levels.

What Is Kept

  • Campaign progress, settings, and locally saved level drafts stay on your device only. They are not tied to the account and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Purchase records (for example, the Remove Ads purchase) are held by Google Play and Apple, not by Ball Launch. They follow the store's own retention rules and let you restore purchases later.
  • Server logs of the deletion itself. They record the internal account identifier and the number of levels and likes removed — no level content, nickname, or email — and are retained for up to 30 days for security and abuse-prevention purposes, then deleted automatically.
